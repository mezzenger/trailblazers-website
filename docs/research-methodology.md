# Research methodology

The first ten names and five category paths came from the original site. The decoded original and all legacy source and image URLs are in `legacy/`. Its Wikipedia-only citations and general image labels were not carried into the publishable records. Biographies were rewritten from institutional sources. Four additional people broaden the first batch beyond the original U.S. focus. For this batch, sources include NASA, the Smithsonian, the U.S. National Archives and Congress, NLM/NIH, Nobel Prize, WHO, national sports bodies, museums, and the Green Belt Movement.

Review process: locate at least two independent credible sources; compare dates, offices, major achievements, and firsts; write a new biography in plain language; record source URLs and a reviewed date; check image rights separately; validate structure and link syntax. A link's appearance in search results or a successful HTTP response proves reachability, not factual support. Each new batch should get a source-level editorial review before publication.

Specific corrections from the legacy page: Kamala Harris served as vice president in 2021–2025 and should not be described as the current vice president; the original image notices did not identify exact licenses; Wikipedia is no longer the only citation. Madam C. J. Walker's millionaire ranking is context-dependent, so the profile describes her documented enterprise without an absolute financial first. Serena Williams's 23 major singles titles are explicitly scoped to the women's Open Era record. Wilma Rudolph's three-gold first is scoped to American women at one Olympic Games.

The records in `pending.json` are discovery names only. They have no biographies, dates, source evidence, or image rights and are excluded from the site and verified total. At the end of Phase 3D, the 20 remaining candidates were individually researched and promoted; `pending.json` is empty. The current validated collection contains 100 profiles and 211 citations. A blank date remains blank when credible references conflict, and inaccessible pages are not treated as evidence.
